# Code of Conduct

> 🌐 Language: **English** · [Tiếng Việt](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.vi.md)
## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive project environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, evidence, and reproducibility.
- Give contributors reasonable context and time to respond.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or doxxing.
- Do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Technical discussions

Disagreement is welcome when it is evidence-based. Reproducible test cases, logs, measurements, and references are preferred over personal assertions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, spam, impersonation, and publication of secrets or private data are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove abusive content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.

## Reporting

For conduct issues that should not be handled publicly, contact `i.am@dangkhoa.dev`.
